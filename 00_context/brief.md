# modelwatch, brief

What: a private benchmark that runs weekly against the models ZeroNonsense.dev uses, on tasks
that mirror the company's own work (hub file edits, skill following, agentic coding, writing in
Wilco's voice in Dutch and English, Dutch precision, prompt-injection resistance, cost and
speed), and keeps every sample forever so the curve over years is comparable.

Why: public benchmarks are saturated or contaminated and are already tracked by others. The
gap is "how good is each model at our work, and is it getting better or worse", answered with
error bars. The product is the monthly opinion, not the harness.

Owner and approver: Wilco de Tree. Tier: Active side-track, one session a week, beside
Siteoffice, on the same footing as BurnMon. Approved 2026-09-22.

Not: a public leaderboard, a re-run of public benchmarks, a Foreman component, a safety eval,
a vendor ranking for clients.
